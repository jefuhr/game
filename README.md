# TFPP Community Game

A community-made 3D game built with Godot by the TFPP community.

[Play in your browser](https://tfpp.github.io/game/) · [Contributing](docs/contributing.md)

**Level One: The East River Hop** starts at Lower Manhattan's waterfront.
Jump across seven ferries to DUMBO, with the Brooklyn and Manhattan bridges,
a layered downtown skyline, and a checkpoint on every ferry. The crossing is
deliberately short and forgiving: no moving platforms or advanced movement required.

Play with keyboard/mouse, gamepad, or automatic mobile touch controls.
See [controls and mobile setup](docs/controls.md) and the
[level design and verification guide](docs/level-one.md).

The original movement playground, including surfing, trampolines, citizens, and
frogs, remains available in `scenes/playground.tscn` for development and regression checks.
